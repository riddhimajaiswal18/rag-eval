Qwen3-Embedding-8B was selected as the embedding model for the RAG retrieval pipeline based on empirical evaluation against competing 
embedding models. The evaluation covered both single-file (single-paper) evidence retrieval and multi-file (multi-paper) evidence retrieval.
 In the single-evidence benchmark, Qwen achieved the highest Recall@1, Recall@5, Recall@10, and MRR@10 among the evaluated models. 
 In the multi-evidence benchmark, where questions could require evidence from multiple papers, Qwen again achieved the highest evidence 
 recall across @1, @5, and @10 among the models evaluated.

The evaluation measured retrieval quality by ranking corpus chunks according to vector similarity against questions with known 
ground-truth evidence. This makes the benchmark directly relevant to selecting the embedding model for the retrieval layer,
while downstream generation quality will be evaluated separately using RAG evaluation methods such as DeepEval.

Benchmark artifact note: the saved ranking outputs predate section-qualified chunk IDs. The evaluation input labels
have since been migrated to the current IDs, but the saved scores and rankings have not been rerun. In particular,
the legacy shared-prefix ID occurred twice in the vLLM paper and cannot be uniquely reconstructed from the old
rankings. Treat the saved metrics as historical until the embedding benchmark is rerun against the current chunks.
